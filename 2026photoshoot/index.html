Family portraits · on the beach · November

Driftwood & golden hour

A coordinated, not matching, palette for thirteen people: warm autumn colors around Julie’s burnt-orange dress, cooled down with sea-blues and soft neutrals that look right against grey-blue water and pale sand. Each look below starts from what that person asked for.

Suggested standing order for the full group. Blues, warm tones and neutrals are spread out so no color clumps in one corner. Illustrations show color and silhouette only.

The palette

Ten colors. Everyone wears one or two of them, plus a neutral, so the photo reads as one family without anyone matching.

Leave at home: pure white and pure black (they blow out or go flat on sand), logos and words, neon, small busy prints, and everyone in identical jeans.

Do bring texture: knit, linen, corduroy, silk, suede. On an overcast November beach, texture gives the photo depth when the light is soft.

Denim: keep it to one or two people (dark wash on Peter and Robert). Mix washes rather than matching.

The looks

Drawn mockups show each person’s colors and outline. Swap a piece freely, as long as it stays inside its color family.

November on the beach

Timing and light

  • Sunset is around 4:55 to 5:10 pm after the clocks change on November 1. Start about 3:45 so the group shots happen in the last good hour.
  • Thin coastal clouds are flattering. They soften faces and turn the water silver-blue, which is what this palette is built for.
  • Face the group slightly away from the sun so nobody squints. Grandma gets the spot with the least glare.

Warmth and comfort

  • Expect 55 to 62 °F with wind. Wear a thin layer under dresses and blouses, and bring a coat for each person to throw on between shots.
  • Barefoot looks best on sand, but cold sand can be hard on bare feet. Neutral flat sandals for anyone who wants them, especially Geri.
  • Bring a folding stool or a blanket so Grandma can sit for the bigger groups.

Bag checklist

  • Lint roller (the fluffy sweater sheds), hair ties and clips for the wind, a small mirror
  • Two neutral blankets, wipes for sandy feet, a towel to stand on while changing shoes
  • Water, snacks for the younger kids, a bag for shoes and jackets
